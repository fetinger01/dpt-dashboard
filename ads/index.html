03 · Track B

Four channels, four weeks

Channels

  • Google Shopping on the Merchant Center already linked, 36 products syncing2
  • YouTube and Discover with the three clips
  • Amazon Sponsored Brands Video on the three sample listings
  • A small Instagram and Facebook test

Budget and reporting

  • A four-week test budget of roughly two to three times the current $50 a week, split across channels1
  • Ad spend charged to the card on the Google Ads account; it can be paused at any time
  • A one-page report every week, with the dashboard live in between
Why now
The Fall sale runs to Dec 31 and the holiday window is open. The campaign already shows people buy when they see the brand.
Nothing misleading
The product in every ad is the real product render, and every claim already appears on the listing today.
Same look everywhere
The ads are cut from the A+ modules, so a shopper who clicks lands on a listing that looks like the ad.

    Google Shopping first, on the Merchant Center already linked, then the clips on YouTube, then Amazon and the Instagram and Facebook test.

Billing stays as agreed. Ad spend is charged to the card on the Google Ads account and can be paused at any time.
